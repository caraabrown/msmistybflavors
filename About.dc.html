About Misty B

Ms. Misty B was born and raised in the gritty city of East Oakland California where the strong survive and the fly thrive. The town swag is one of a kind from our music and food to our fashion. That unique culture is what Miss Misty B is here to bring you. She represents Dreamchasers who know they're built for greatness.
